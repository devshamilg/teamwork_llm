Features

Everything your team needs to talk and get things done

A focused workspace for group conversations, with AI available exactly when you need it.

Shared rooms

Spin up a room for every project, team or topic and keep conversations organised in one place.

Real-time messaging

Messages are delivered instantly over WebSockets, so everyone stays in sync without refreshing.

AI assistant

Type @AI followed by your question and the answer lands in the chat for everyone.

Invite teammates

Add people by username or email. They get a notification and can jump straight into the conversation.

Private by default

Only members can read a room. Access is checked on every request and every live connection.

Your profile

Set your display name and bio so teammates know who they are talking to.

AI assistant

An extra teammate that’s always available

No separate tool, no copy-pasting. Ask the AI right in the conversation and everyone sees the same answer.

  • Context aware. The AI reads the recent messages in the room before it replies.
  • One mention away. Start any message with @AI to call it.
  • Shared answers. Replies are posted to the room, so the whole team benefits.
